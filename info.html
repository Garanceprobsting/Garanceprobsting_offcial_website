Work Info

Garance Pröbsting is a French product designer based in Berlin, developing objects, furniture, systems and spatial installations. Her practice has developed through experiences across France, Germany and Australia, combining a background in product design with hands-on experience in manufacturing and production. Rooted in an education at the School of Applied Arts in Paris, she is currently pursuing her master’s degree at the Berlin University of the Arts. Working primarily with metal, alongside textiles and other materials, Garance dedicates her practice to producing pieces meant to become mediators of our daily rituals, active presences shaping the way we experience materials and inhabit our spaces.
