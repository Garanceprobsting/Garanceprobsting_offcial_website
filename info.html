Work Info

Garance is a French product designer based in Berlin, developping objects, furniture, systems and spatial experiences. Rooted in an education at the School of Applied Arts in Paris (ENSAAMA Olivier de Serres), she is currently pursuing her master’s degree at the University of the Arts (UdK) in Berlin. Garance dedicates her practice to producing pieces meant to become mediators of our daily rituals, active presences shaping the way we experience materials and inhabit our spaces.
