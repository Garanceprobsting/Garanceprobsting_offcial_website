L'oiseau

February 2024

This sandstone water carafe is composed of an ergonomic handle, which follows the natural movement of the hand while serving water, making it easy to fill it even in small sinks. By adapting to limited environments, the carafe responds to a common but often overlooked challenge of daily life in compact living spaces.
