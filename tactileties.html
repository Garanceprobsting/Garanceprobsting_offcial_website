Tactile Ties

June 2025

Tactile Ties is a daily mediator, designed to reconcile relationships and enable a ritualized communication within members of a household. The object invites users to create their own visual language through magnetic modules, coded signage, and a system for measuring emotional, physical, and social availability. Crafted from durable 430 stainless steel, this wall installation fosters meaningful connections through its presence in the space and use across time.
